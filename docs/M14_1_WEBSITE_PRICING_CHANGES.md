## Approved product model

- **Regstead Scan — £0.** Free baseline scan; no subscription required.
- **Regstead Monitor — £16.99 / month.** Continuous evidence-led website monitoring for one primary website.
- Recurring monthly subscription; cancel anytime. Cancellation at period end retains access for the paid period and ends future monitoring when cancellation becomes effective; historical reports are retained.
- Rejoining uses the currently configured Monitor subscription. There is one paid product.

**Tax decision still required:** 1699 pence is the amount before separately configured Stripe tax behaviour. Do not publish an assertion that this includes or excludes VAT until approved. Ensure public wording, Stripe settings and invoices match before live charges. Automatic Stripe Tax has not been enabled.
